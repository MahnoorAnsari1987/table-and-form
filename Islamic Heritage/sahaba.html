The Companions

As-Sahaba

Selected companions of Prophet Muhammad ﷺ

A note of adab

The Sahaba (Companions) are those who met the Prophet ﷺ as believers and died upon faith. Muslims traditionally mention them with honor: radiyallahu 'anhu (may Allah be pleased with him). These short profiles follow well-known biographical outlines from classical sources such as Ibn Sa'd and later compilations, without invented speeches.

حضرت ابو بکر صدیقؓ
Caliph

Abu Bakr as-Siddiq (may Allah be pleased with him)

A close friend of the Prophet ﷺ and the first caliph. Known for early belief, companionship on the Hijrah, and leadership during a fragile moment after the Prophet's passing.

  • Preserved communal unity in a time of crisis
  • Commissioned the first collected written mushaf under Zayd ibn Thabit
حضرت عمر فاروقؓ
Caliph

Umar ibn al-Khattab (may Allah be pleased with him)

The second caliph, remembered for administrative reform, a reputation for justice, and the expansion of the early state. The Hijri calendar's official use is associated with his era in historical reports.

  • Developed diwan registers and city planning
  • Known in tradition for accessibility and accountability
حضرت عثمان غنیؓ
Caliph

Uthman ibn Affan (may Allah be pleased with him)

The third caliph, among the earliest Muslims and a son-in-law of the Prophet ﷺ. His era is closely linked to the standardized written Qur'an distributed to major cities.

  • Uthmanic codex project, reported in Sahih al-Bukhari
  • Known for generosity, including support for communal water in Madinah
حضرت علیؓ
Caliph

Ali ibn Abi Talib (may Allah be pleased with him)

Cousin and son-in-law of the Prophet ﷺ, raised in his household. The fourth caliph, widely remembered for courage, eloquence, and deep knowledge of the Qur'an.

  • Central figure in early Islamic political history
  • A major transmitter of knowledge to later generations
حضرت بلالؓ
Devotion

Bilal ibn Rabah (may Allah be pleased with him)

An early Muslim remembered for steadfastness under persecution and for giving the adhan in Madinah. His life is a frequently cited example of dignity in faith.

  • Among the first muezzins of the community
  • A symbol of equality in the early ummah
حضرت عبدالرحمن بن عوفؓ
Companion

Abdur Rahman ibn Awf (may Allah be pleased with him)

A distinguished companion of Prophet Muhammad ﷺ and one of the ten companions promised Paradise. He was known for his generosity and devotion.

  • One of the ten companions promised Paradise
  • Known for generosity and charitable giving
حضرت خالد بن ولیدؓ
Companion

Khalid ibn al-Walid (may Allah be pleased with him)

A renowned companion of Prophet Muhammad ﷺ and a distinguished military commander of the early Muslim community. He was known as the Sword of Allah.

  • Known for exceptional military leadership
  • Called the Sword of Allah in authentic hadith
حضرت سعد بن ابی وقاصؓ
Companion

Sa'd ibn Abi Waqqas (may Allah be pleased with him)

A prominent companion of Prophet Muhammad ﷺ and one of the ten companions promised Paradise. He was known for his courage and important role in the early Muslim community.

  • One of the ten companions promised Paradise
  • Known for courage and leadership
